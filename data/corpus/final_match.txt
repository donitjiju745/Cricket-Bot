The final match was played at Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad between India and Australia. India scored 240 runs in 50 overs with Virat Kohli scoring 54 and KL Rahul scoring 66. Australia chased down the target of 241 in 43 overs. Travis Head scored a brilliant 137 runs and was awarded Man of the Match. Pat Cummins was the winning captain for Australia.
